undo-deleteCollection screen · records

Delete is immediate and undoable

No confirmation dialogs. Card trash, row menu Delete and batch Delete hide the records and show the outcome toast with Undo. Nothing is written until the toast leaves (5 s), the user leaves the collection, or the app goes to the background. Theme switch (top right) shows Latte and Mocha.

Motion
Collapse height + fade, 200 ms
The gap has to close anyway, so the height collapse is the message; a sideways slide implies a swipe that doesn't exist. Exit: fade 140 ms while height eases to 0 in 200 ms. Enter: the reverse, then a 1.4 s accent tint fades out on restored items. Reduced motion: instant, tint kept (it's color, not motion).
Countdown
Plain toast, no progress line
A draining line turns a reassurance into a race. The timer pauses on hover/focus and restarts at 5 s on each new delete, so the user is never cut off. Compare with the “countdown” tweak on the live frames.
Row menu label
“Delete”, no ellipsis
An ellipsis promises a further step; there isn't one now. Same for the selection bar. Spanish: “Eliminar”.

Behavior notes
Pending deletes add up in one toast; Undo restores all of them to their original positions (they're hidden in place, never moved).
Commit happens on toast timeout, Back / leaving the collection, or app backgrounded. Commit is silent; on failure the records re-enter and the banner replaces the toast.
The toast is role="status"; the count change is announced. Timer pauses while hovered or focused. Focus moves to the next card after a delete, and to the first restored card after Undo.
