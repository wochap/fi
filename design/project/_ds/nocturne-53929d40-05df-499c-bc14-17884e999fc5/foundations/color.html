Usage

Reach for the theme-relative roles first — --color-accent-fill / -edge / -text / -ink / -ink-strong and --color-neutral-fill / -fill-strong / -edge / -ghost / -muted — they point at the right ramp step for the active theme. Use numbered steps only when a value must stay fixed across themes. Status colors are the Catppuccin red, green and yellow; they carry marks, icons and short labels, with body text staying in --color-text. Paragraph-size accent text is --color-accent-text (accent-300 on Mocha, accent-700 on Latte).
