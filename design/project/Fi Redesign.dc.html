Shared components & rules
rulesRules
Rules
  1. Choice control by option count: 2–4 segmented · 5–10 dropdown (picker sheet on mobile) · more than 10 searchable.
  2. Clear (✕) only on optional fields that have a value.
  3. Optional boolean is Yes / No / unset. Unset slider shows a dashed track and Set.
  4. Errors appear after the first save, with a summary and Show above the footer. Needed = dashed border plus label.
  5. Mobile: one field per row, touch targets ≥ 44px, list rows ≥ 64px.
  6. Switches: 38×22 on desktop, 44×26 on mobile.
  7. Menus on desktop, action sheets on mobile.
  8. Field status is never shown by colour alone: always an icon or label too.
  9. Fading dividers only between rows inside cards.
  10. Confirm dialogs: the destructive action is secondary on the left; the safe choice is primary on the right. Nothing has undo.
  11. “?” help buttons sit beside non-obvious controls and open a dismissible popover (desktop) or sheet (mobile).
